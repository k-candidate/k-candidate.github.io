What is the difference between a "*complicated*" problem and a "*complex*" one?

**Complicated** means something has **many parts** and is **hard to understand**, but it **can be solved with rules, logic, and expert steps**.

**Complex** means something has **many interconnected parts that change each other**, making the **final outcome unpredictable**.

See [https://www.sfu.ca/complex-systems-frameworks/applications/complex-and-complicated-are-not-the-same.html](https://www.sfu.ca/complex-systems-frameworks/applications/complex-and-complicated-are-not-the-same.html) for a little more information.
